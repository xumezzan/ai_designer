# Sculpted Garden collection artwork

`portrait.webp` (1024 × 1536, 137,830 bytes) and `wide.webp` (1536 × 1024,
119,378 bytes) are the original Ivory Atelier collection artwork generated on
2026-10-21 with the built-in ImageGen tool. They were copied unchanged from
`ivory-atelier-prototype/dist/assets/invitation-garden*.webp`; the original
project's `ASSETS.md` records the complete prompts and source PNG paths.

The prompt requested original porcelain/plaster magnolias, restrained gold
stamens and silk ribbons on a continuous ivory surface, with quiet space for
live typography. It explicitly excluded paper, cards, mockups, frames, text,
logos and interfaces. Portrait flowers enter from upper-right and lower-left;
the landscape variant places flowers on the right. They are opaque WebP
derivatives encoded with Sharp quality 85, effort 6, preserving composition.

These are full-bleed art inside the invitation hero. Material volume is baked
into 2D pixels, not an interactive 3D model. New AI-generated artwork belongs to
its invitation and uses the private platform media store; these two collection
files are not returned as a successful substitute when a provider fails.
